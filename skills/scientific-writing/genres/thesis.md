# Genre: thesis

**Reader**: a committee and the next student, reading to learn the
method well enough to extend it.

**Assumed**: less than in a paper. The next student knows the field but
not this group's tools, conventions or unpublished results, so those are
introduced where a paper would cite them.

## Rules

- **Derivations go inline**, per `math`'s "Derive it where it is used".
  A chapter that cites its own appendix at every step teaches nothing.
- **Chapters may restate the setup they depend on.** A chapter is read
  on its own, so restating a model or notation is context, not a
  repeated claim. The repetition audit applies to claims, not setup.
- **Evidence routing still applies.** A figure is still not narrated,
  and the visibility question still decides between changing the figure
  and writing prose. More context does not license reciting the plot.

## Budgets

The core paragraph and caption defaults hold. Inline derivations do not
count against the paragraph budget: displayed equations are excluded
from `prose-metrics` paragraphs, and the prose between them states each
step's warrant.
